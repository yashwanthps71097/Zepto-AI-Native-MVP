## 📏 Specifications & Typography Hierarchy

All text elements are optimized for presentation-quality readability on large slide decks (1920×1080 Figma scale):

- **Main Screen Title:** `38px` Extra Bold
- **Section Headers (e.g., Shop by Category, AI Picks):** `30px` Bold
- **Category & Product Labels:** `26px` Medium
- **Line Heights:** `140%` for crisp paragraph reading
- **Design Elements:** Rounded cards with `16–20px` radius, soft drop shadows, and a clean purple-and-white theme.
